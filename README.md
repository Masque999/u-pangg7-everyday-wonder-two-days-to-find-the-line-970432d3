# Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line

One original sentence, at most 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life.

Entry: `The puddle outside my office holds the whole sky, and every morning I step over the universe on my way to a meeting about meetings.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`).
